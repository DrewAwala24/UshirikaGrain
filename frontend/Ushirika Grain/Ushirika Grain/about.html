About Ushirika Grain

Grain you can trust, from gate to payout

Ushirika Grain is Swahili for beautiful. We want harvest records, storage and payouts to be just that: clear, fair and easy to check.

Why we built it

One shared record for the whole cooperative

In grain-growing regions such as Trans Nzoia, cooperatives collect harvests from many smallholders across several sub-counties. During harvest season, records are often kept on paper and spread across depots.

Managers cannot see how full each depot is. Wet grain can sit unnoticed. A farmer who loses a ticket has little proof of what was delivered, and payouts slow down while everyone argues about the numbers.

Ushirika Grain puts extension officers, cooperative managers and farmers in front of the same record, so decisions are made on facts.

What it does

Six tools that work together

Intake registry

Log the farmer, sub-county, produce, bags and moisture reading as the grain arrives at the depot.

Depot capacity

See stored volume against silo limits for every depot, and which ones are close to full.

Capacity forecast

A 14-day projection for each depot shows when it will fill at the current pace of deliveries.

Spoilage risk

Each batch is scored from its moisture and the days it has been in store, with a recommended action.

Digital audit trail

Every deposit gets a tracking ID and is signed against the record before it, so edits cannot hide.

Receipt lookup

Farmers enter their tracking ID to see the weight, moisture and payout status of their deposit.

The AI layer

Predictions you can check by hand

Both predictions use simple, transparent rules, so a manager can see why the system is asking for action.

Spoilage risk score

Every batch gets a score from 0 to 100. Moisture above 13.5% pushes it up quickly, and wet grain adds more risk for each day it stays in store. A score of 65 or more is marked high.

Capacity forecast

We average the last seven days of intake at each depot and divide the space left by that pace. The dashboard draws the result as a 14-day projection.

Smart alerts

When a depot is 90% full or has under three days of space, Ushirika Grain flags it and suggests the depot with the most room, with an SMS you can copy.

Our principles

What we hold ourselves to

Farmer first

A farmer should always be able to see what they delivered, when, and whether they have been paid.

Simple at the gate

Intake takes under a minute on a phone, because the depot gate is not the place for complicated software.

Evidence you can check

Records are signed and linked, so anyone can verify that nothing was changed after the fact.
