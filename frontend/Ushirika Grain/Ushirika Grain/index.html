The problem

Three things that go wrong every harvest season

Grain arrives from many smallholders at once. Paper records and guesswork cannot keep up.

Nobody can see how full a depot is

Managers find out about an overflowing silo when a truck is already at the gate, and farmers are turned away.

Live capacity for every depot, plus a 14-day forecast and a suggestion of where to send the next delivery.

Wet grain spoils before anyone notices

Grain stored above the safe moisture limit can grow mould. By the time it shows, the whole lot may be lost.

Every batch gets a spoilage risk score from moisture and days in store, with a clear next step.

Paper receipts get lost, and payouts get disputed

A missing ticket means arguments over weight and quality, and farmers wait longer to be paid.

A unique tracking ID for each deposit that farmers can look up at any time, signed so it cannot be changed quietly.

How it works

From the depot gate to the farmer's phone

Step 1

Record at the gate

The extension officer logs the farmer, bags and moisture reading. Ushirika Grain checks depot space and scores spoilage risk before the grain is stored.

Step 2

Watch every depot

The dashboard shows live capacity, a 14-day forecast and alerts that say which depot should take the next truck.

Step 3

Farmers check their receipt

A tracking ID replaces the paper ticket. Each deposit is signed against the one before it, so records cannot be changed without showing.

For farmers

Check your deposit in seconds

No account needed. Type the tracking ID from your ticket to see what you delivered and where your payout stands.
